# BizEcon study site

An interactive, single-page study site for Business Economics, Lectures 1–7 (Imperial College Business School). It covers consumer theory, demand and elasticity, costs, supply and equilibrium, perfect competition and policy, monopoly, and oligopoly and game theory.

- Six live models: Cobb-Douglas demand, elasticity and revenue, cost curves, the shutdown decision, caps and taxes, monopoly
- A playbook of 27 step-by-step methods, one per problem-set question type
- An endless practice generator with full working
- Quizzes, flashcards, a formula sheet and common mark-losing traps
- Problem-set answers stay hidden until you reveal them

Everything is in `index.html`, including the maths rendering, so it works offline.
